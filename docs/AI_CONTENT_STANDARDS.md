# AI Content Standards - iiskills.cloud Learn Math

## Overview

All curriculum, modules, quizzes, and educational content in the **iiskills.cloud Learn Math** platform are **AI-generated** to ensure consistency, quality, and alignment with Indian academic standards.

## Content Generation Framework

### 1. Curriculum Alignment

All AI-generated content follows these standards:

#### CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education)
- Mathematics curriculum for Classes 6-12
- Aligned with latest CBSE syllabus guidelines
- Covers core mathematical concepts and applications
- Follows CBSE assessment patterns

#### ICSE (Indian Certificate of Secondary Education)
- Comprehensive mathematics coverage
- Problem-solving focus
- Application-based questions
- Theory and practical balance

#### NEP 2020 (National Education Policy 2020)
- Competency-based learning approach
- Critical thinking and problem-solving
- Multi-disciplinary learning
- Flexibility in learning paths

#### Quiz Generation

- **Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)**
